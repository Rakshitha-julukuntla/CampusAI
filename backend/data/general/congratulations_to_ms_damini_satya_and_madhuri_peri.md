# Congratulations to Ms Damini Satya and Madhuri Peri – BVRIT HYDERABAD College of Engineering for Women

Source: https://bvrithyderabad.edu.in/congratulations-to-ms-damini-satya-and-madhuri-peri

FOSSASIA: Damini Satya student of 2012 batch represented BVRITH in FOSSASIA, Singopore in 2016. FOSSASIA is a forum where people over the world present their ideas towards developing software application for social change using a wide range of technologies. With her guidance final year students Madhuri Peri and Sushma presented their ideas in 2017.
