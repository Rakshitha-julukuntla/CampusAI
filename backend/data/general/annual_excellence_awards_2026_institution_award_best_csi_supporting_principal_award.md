# Annual Excellence Awards -2026 : Institution Award- Best CSI Supporting Principal Award – BVRIT HYDERABAD College of Engineering for Women

Source: https://bvrithyderabad.edu.in/annual-excellence-awards-2026-institution-award-best-csi-supporting-principal-award

Dr. K. V. N. Sunitha , Principal BVRIT HYDERABAD College of Engineering for Women, Hyderabad, Telangana received the prestigious “ Institution Award- Best CSI Supporting Principal Award ”. This well-deserved recognition is a testament to Dr. K. V. N. Sunitha’s outstanding dedication, exemplary service, and significant contributions to the academic and professional community.
