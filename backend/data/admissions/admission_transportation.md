# Transportation – BVRIT HYDERABAD College of Engineering for Women

Source: https://bvrithyderabad.edu.in/admission/transportation

#### Our institution believes that efficient ways of transportation in turn results in better productivity.

Thus BVRIT provides 15 buses for private transportation from all over the city to the campus. From over 15 different routes , the fleet carries students across Hyderabad and nearby to the campus. All the buses are equipped with GPRS system for tracking and safety of the students. The timings and stops are detailed below.
