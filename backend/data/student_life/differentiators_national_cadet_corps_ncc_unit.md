### BVRITH NCC

BVRIT Hyderabad proudly marked a historic milestone with the official raising of its , strengthening the institution’s commitment to developing leadership, discipline, patriotism, and social responsibility among students.

The NCC Unit Raising Ceremony was conducted on 04 December 2025 in the presence of distinguished officers from the National Cadet Corps. Col. Chekuri Srinivas Rao and Maj. Revathi P graced the occasion and formally inaugurated the NCC unit, encouraging students to actively participate in NCC activities and contribute to nation-building.

The establishment of the NCC unit provides students with opportunities to undergo military-oriented training, adventure activities, community service, leadership development, and personality enhancement. Cadets will also have the opportunity to participate in national camps, social awareness programmes, and disaster management initiatives under the guidance of the NCC.

The Management, Principal, faculty members, and students expressed their gratitude to the NCC authorities for extending the opportunity to establish the NCC unit at BVRIT Hyderabad. The occasion marks the beginning of a new chapter in empowering students to uphold the NCC motto, “Unity and Discipline,” while serving society and the nation with dedication.

The college looks forward to nurturing responsible citizens and future leaders through the newly established NCC unit. The event was attended by faculty members, students, and invited guests, making it a memorable milestone in the institution’s journey.
