# Rotaract Club – BVRIT HYDERABAD College of Engineering for Women

Source: https://bvrithyderabad.edu.in/student-activities/rotaract-club

## Rotaract Club of BVRIT Hyderabad.

The Rotaract Club of our college is a vibrant student-led organization dedicated to fostering leadership, community service, and professional development. As a part of the global Rotaract network under Rotary International, our club empowers students to become compassionate leaders who create meaningful and lasting change in society.

AIM:

At the beginning of every Rotaract year, the club organizes its Orientation and Installation Ceremony, a significant event that welcomes new members and officially inducts the incoming board. The ceremony includes the presentation of the club’s annual report, recognition of members for their contributions, the oath-taking of the newly elected leadership team, and inspiring addresses by distinguished guests. This event reflects the club’s commitment to continuity, leadership, fellowship, and the Rotary motto of “Service Above Self.”

OBJECTIVE:

As part of our commitment to environmental sustainability, the club conducted a Seed Ball Making Drive, where members prepared seed balls using a mixture of soil and seeds and distributed them across various locations on the college campus to encourage afforestation and promote a greener environment. During Janmashtami, the club organized a festive fundraising initiative by selling traditional sweets across the campus. To celebrate the vibrant festival of Navaratri, the club conducted a Garba Workshop, providing students with an opportunity to experience the cultural significance of Garba in campus life. The funds raised through the Janmashtami initiative were utilized to organize a Stationery Distribution Drive, through which essential educational supplies were donated to children in orphanages

MOTTO:

Through these initiatives, the Rotaract Club continues to inspire students to become socially responsible leaders, promote environmental awareness, celebrate cultural diversity, and create a meaningful impact within the community through service and fellowship.
