# Distinguished Lecture -“AIR FLOW MANAGEMENT  in Clean Rooms and Laboratories”  – BVRIT HYDERABAD College of Engineering for Women

Source: https://bvrithyderabad.edu.in/distinguished-lecture-air-flow-management-in-clean-rooms-and-laboratories

BVRIT HYDERABAD College of Engineering for Women, Hyderabad has received the prestigious “ Best CSI Supporting Principal Award ”. The award was received by the principal Dr.K.V.N Sunitha from The Computer Society of India (CSI), Hyderabad Chapter.

This recognition is a tribute to the institution for the unwavering commitment in fostering a culture of academic excellence, research, and the practical applications of engineering principles.
